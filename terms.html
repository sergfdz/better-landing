02

What Bet-ter is

Bet-ter connects to your bank account to detect gambling-related transactions, track your streak and risk patterns, and optionally notify a trusted contact when new activity is detected. It's a personal accountability tool.

Bet-ter is not: a bank, a lender, a licensed gambling-treatment provider, a medical or financial advisor, or an official self-exclusion registry. It doesn't block transactions, freeze accounts, or prevent gambling directly — it detects and reports on activity that has already happened, based on data your bank makes available.

If you're struggling with gambling, please also reach out to a licensed professional or a dedicated helpline in your country — Bet-ter is meant to complement that support, not replace it.

Bank connection

We rely on Plaid and your bank for the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of transaction data — we can't guarantee that every transaction, or every gambling transaction specifically, will be detected or classified correctly, including because a merchant's name doesn't clearly indicate gambling, or because of a delay or outage on your bank's or Plaid's side.

06

Self-exclusion feature

Bet-ter links you to official, government- or operator-run self-exclusion programs for your country. We don't operate, control, or verify enrollment in any of these programs — confirming you've self-excluded inside Bet-ter is a personal record for your own tracking, not a legal enrollment, and has no effect on any external registry.
